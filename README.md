# abyss-docs: multiplanetary systems development

This repository is my **public engineering log** — a transparent, first-principles record of every project, failure, lesson, and breakthrough on the long road toward building humanity's multiplanetary future.

Started in 2026 at age 17, "Abyss-Docs" documents the daily reality of self-directed learning and hardware development. It serves as both a personal engineering journal and a long-term proof of consistent mission drive.

### Purpose
- Document **real engineering work** transparently: polished successes, painful failures, dead-ends, and solutions.
- Practice rigorous critical thinking, systems-level understanding, and first-principles reasoning.
- Show the full hero’s journey — the actual process, not just highlight reels.
- Develop professionalism through clear documentation, honest self-assessment, and structured communication.

### What You Will Find Here
- **Project Code** (Arduino, Python, C++, etc.)
- **CAD Files** (Autodesk Inventor, Fusion 360, and future tools)
- **Machining & Manufacturing Files** (when applicable)
- **Detailed Engineering Logs** — daily and weekly entries covering:
  - Physics derivations and first-principles breakdowns
  - Design decisions, trade-offs, and systems interactions
  - How components and files work together as a cohesive system
  - Performance data, efficiency analysis, and failure root causes
  - Lessons learned and concrete improvements for the next iteration

### Content Style
All documentation emphasizes **systems thinking**: how electrons, materials, code, mechanics, and physics interact.

Long-form (10–20 minute) video documentation of engineering and personal context is posted on my [YouTube channel](https://youtube.com/@Cailen_B).

### Current Focus
Electromagnetic mass driver / coilgun prototypes as the foundational project. These early builds build core competence for future work on lunar mass drivers, robotic construction swarms, ISRU systems, and Mars surface development (Arcadia).

### Long-Term Vision
To contribute meaningfully to lunar infrastructure — especially mass drivers for efficient payload launch — and sustainable Mars city construction. This repository stands as a public record of years of deliberate, daily effort toward that goal.
